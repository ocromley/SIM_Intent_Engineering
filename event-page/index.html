Panel Discussion: Intent Engineering

How this emerging approach is changing the way organizations think about AI

From Instructions to Intent

Traditional approaches to AI focus on giving systems instructions. Intent Engineering moves beyond that — defining what we want AI to accomplish and measuring results against goals. Join us for a conversation on how this shift is reshaping the way organizations design, deploy, and evaluate AI solutions.

What we'll explore:

  • What Intent Engineering is and why it matters now
  • Moving from giving AI instructions to defining outcomes and goals
  • How organizations measure AI results against intended goals
  • Practical ways technology leaders can apply an intent-first mindset to AI initiatives
  • Real-world perspectives from leaders working at the forefront of AI

"Whether your organization is early in its AI journey or scaling advanced initiatives, this discussion will offer practical insight and valuable executive perspective."

About SIM Chicago

Society for Information Management

SIM is the premier network for IT leaders, comprised of over 5,000 members across 40 chapters in the United States. For over five decades, SIM has been the leading organization for senior IT executives, providing a unique forum for networking, professional development, and thought leadership.

Our Mission:

  • To nurture a community of senior IT executives who support each other in their professional growth
  • To foster meaningful connections between IT leaders across industries
  • To advance the IT profession through knowledge sharing and mentorship
  • To promote diversity and inclusion in technology leadership

SIM Chicago Chapter:

The Chicago chapter is one of SIM's most active communities, hosting regular events that bring together CIOs, CTOs, and senior technology leaders from the region's top organizations.
